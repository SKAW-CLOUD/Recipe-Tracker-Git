🥘 Preparation Steps
1. Boil the tubers

Wash cocoyams or yams thoroughly.

Boil until completely soft.

2. Peel while warm

Remove skin carefully. Keep the tubers warm.

3. Pound into dough

Pound boiled tubers in a mortar or mash in a food processor until smooth and elastic.

The dough should be soft but firm enough to hold shape.

4. Prepare the proteins

Boil beef/cow skin/tripe with salt and stock cubes until tender.

Add smoked fish near the end so it softens.

5. Prepare the vegetables

Wash and chop waterleaf (or spinach).

6. Combine

In a pot, mix the pounded cocoyam with palm oil.

Add chopped vegetables and proteins.

Stir gently over low heat until everything is well combined.

Adjust seasoning with salt, pepper, and stock cubes.
