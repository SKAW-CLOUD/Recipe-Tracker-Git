# Achu and Yellow Soup

## Ingredients

-   Poundo cocoyams
-   1 cup palm oil
-   1 tbsp limestone (kanwa)
-   Meat stock
-   Spices: country onion, pepper, salt

## Instructions

1.  Pound cocoyams into a smooth paste.
2.  Mix palm oil and limestone until yellow.
3.  Add warm meat stock to make soup.
4.  Season with spices.
5.  Serve soup with achu balls.
