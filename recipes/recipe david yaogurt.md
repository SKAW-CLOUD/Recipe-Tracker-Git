Here’s a **very short summary** for making yogurt:

1. Heat milk to **~85°C (185°F)**, then cool to **45°C (113°F)**.
2. Mix in a spoonful of yogurt (starter).
3. Keep warm **6–12 hours** until thick.
4. Chill in the fridge.
