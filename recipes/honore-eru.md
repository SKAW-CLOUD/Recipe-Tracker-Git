# Eru (Afang Soup)

## Ingredients

-   2 cups shredded Eru/Afang leaves
-   3 cups waterleaf/spinach
-   1 lb assorted meat
-   1 cup smoked fish
-   1 cup palm oil
-   Seasoning cubes and salt

## Instructions

1.  Cook meat and fish together.
2.  Add waterleaf and allow to wilt.
3.  Add eru and mix thoroughly.
4.  Pour in palm oil and simmer.
5.  Season and serve with garri or fufu.
