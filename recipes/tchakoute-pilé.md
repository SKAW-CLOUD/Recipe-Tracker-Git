🥘 Steps to Prepare Pilé
1. Boil the tubers

Wash the cocoyams or yams thoroughly.

Boil in enough water until very soft (poke with a fork; should slide easily).

2. Peel while warm

Let them cool slightly, then peel the skin off.

Keep them warm for pounding.

3. Pound into smooth dough

Option 1: Traditional mortar & pestle

Pound the boiled tubers gradually until smooth, stretchy, and elastic.

Add a little water if needed for softness.

Option 2: Food processor (shortcut)

Cut boiled tubers into chunks.

Blend or mash until smooth.

Transfer to a bowl and knead by hand to get elastic texture.

4. Shape the Pilé

Roll into balls or mounds using wet hands.
