🥘 How to Prepare It
🥔 PART 1 — Making the Achu
1. Boil the cocoyams

Wash the cocoyams well (do not peel).

Boil until completely soft.

Boil the plantain separately.

2. Peel the cocoyams

Remove the skin while still warm (not hot).

3. Pound the cocoyam

Pound gradually in a mortar until smooth and stretchy.

Add small pieces of plantain while pounding (optional).

Pound until it becomes a smooth, elastic dough.

4. Shape the Achu

Roll into round balls and place in a warm container so it stays soft.

🍲 PART 2 — Making the Yellow Soup
1. Prepare your meat stock

Boil your preferred meat (beef, goat meat, cow skin).
Season with salt, cubes, and pepper.
Keep about 2 cups of the stock.

2. Dissolve the kanwa

Put a tiny piece (be careful: too much ruins the taste)

Add a little warm water and stir

You’ll see the water turn cloudy—this is what activates the yellow color

3. Mix the soup

In a large bowl:

Add the palm oil

Add the kanwa water (little by little)

Stir continuously until the oil turns bright yellow

Add the warm meat stock

Add achu spice

Add salt/cubes to taste

The soup should be:

Light

Smooth

Yellow

Not too thick

If it’s too thick → add more stock
If it’s too watery → add a bit more palm oil

4. Add the meat

Put the cooked meat into the soup.
