# Puff-Puff

## Ingredients

-   3 cups flour
-   1 cup sugar
-   2 tsp yeast
-   Warm water
-   Oil for frying

## Instructions

1.  Mix flour, yeast, sugar, and water into sticky dough.
2.  Allow dough to rise for 1 hour.
3.  Heat oil and drop small scoops of dough.
4.  Fry golden brown.
5.  Drain and serve.
