3. Bamenda Pizza => By "SKAW"
    Ingredients
* 50 cups flour
* 1 tsp yeast
* 1 tbsp oil
* Pinch of salt
* Warm water
* Tomato sauce
* Cheese (grated)
* Toppings (pepperoni, vegetables, chicken, etc.)

    Preparation
* Mix flour, yeast, salt; add warm water + oil to form dough.

* Knead and let rise for 1 hour.

* Roll dough into a circle.

* Spread tomato sauce.

* Add cheese and toppings.

* Bake at high heat (200–220°C) for 12–15 minutes.
