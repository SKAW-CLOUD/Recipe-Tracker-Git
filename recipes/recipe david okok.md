Ah, **Okok**! That’s a delicious Cameroonian dish, usually made with **okok leaves** (also called *cocoyam leaves* in some areas) and often cooked with fish, beef, or other protein. Here’s a **short and simple version**:

---

### **Ingredients** (serves 4)

* 2 cups okok leaves (washed and chopped)
* 1–2 smoked fish or meat (beef/chicken)
* 1 onion, chopped
* 2–3 palm oil tbsp (or vegetable oil)
* 2–3 chili peppers (optional)
* 1–2 tomatoes, chopped
* Salt and seasoning cubes to taste

---

### **Method (Summary)**

1. Heat oil, sauté onion, chili, and tomatoes.
2. Add your protein (smoked fish, beef, or chicken) and cook a few minutes.
3. Add chopped okok leaves, salt, and seasoning.
4. Add a little water, cover, and simmer **15–20 min** until leaves are tender.
5. Serve hot with **fufu, plantains, or rice**.
