🥘 How to Prepare Eru (Step-By-Step)
1. Prepare the meat/fish

Wash and boil the beef/skin/tripe with salt and seasoning cubes

Add water as needed and cook until soft

Add the smoked or dry fish so it softens too

👉 Keep the meat stock (you’ll need it!)

2. Prepare the vegetables

Wash the waterleaf (or spinach) and chop it

No need to wash eru too much because it can get tough—just rinse lightly

Tip: If eru is very dry, soak it in warm water for a few minutes to soften it.

3. Start cooking

Put your cooked meat + stock in a pot

Add the chopped waterleaf/spinach

Let it cook for 5–10 minutes until it releases water

4. Add eru

Gradually add the eru leaves to the pot

Stir continuously

The mixture should be thick, not watery

Let it cook for 10–15 minutes

5. Add flavor

Pour in the palm oil until the eru turns a shiny green

Add crayfish

Add pepper and adjust salt

Stir very well so everything combines

6. Final simmer

Reduce heat and let it simmer 10 more minutes

Eru should look glossy and not soupy
