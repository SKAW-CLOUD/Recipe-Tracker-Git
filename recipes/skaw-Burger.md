4. Burger => By "SKAW"
    Ingredients
* Burger buns
* Ground beef or chicken patty
* Salt and pepper
* Lettuce
* Tomato slices
* Onion slices
* Cheese slices
* Ketchup or mayonnaise

    Preparation

* Season the patty with salt and pepper.

* Grill or fry until fully cooked.

* Toast the burger buns lightly.

* Assemble: bun → patty → cheese → vegetables → sauce → top bun.

Serve immediately !
