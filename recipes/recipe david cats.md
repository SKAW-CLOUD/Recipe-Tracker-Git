Here’s a **short summary** for cooking rabbit stew:

1. Brown rabbit pieces in oil.
2. Sauté onion, garlic, peppers, and tomatoes.
3. Add spices (thyme, paprika, salt, pepper).
4. Return rabbit, add water/stock, simmer **45–60 min**.
5. Garnish and serve.
