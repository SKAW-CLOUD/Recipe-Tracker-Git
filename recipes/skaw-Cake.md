2. Simple Cake => By "SKAW"
   Ingredients
* 2 cups flour
* 1 cup sugar
* 1 cup butter
* 3 eggs
* 1 cup milk
* 1 tsp baking powder
* 1 tsp vanilla flavor

    Preparation
* Mix butter and sugar until creamy.

* Add eggs one at a time and mix.

* Add flour + baking powder, mix gently.

* Add milk and vanilla; mix into smooth batter.

* Pour into a greased baking pan.

* Bake at 180°C for 25–35 minutes.
