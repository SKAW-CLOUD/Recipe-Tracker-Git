🥘 Preparation Steps
1. Prepare the meat/protein

Wash and cut beef/cow skin/tripe into bite-size pieces

Boil with salt, pepper, and stock cubes until tender

Add smoked fish or shrimp near the end so it softens

2. Prepare the bitterleaf

Wash thoroughly (to remove excess bitterness)

Chop finely

3. Make peanut sauce

If using raw peanuts: grind into paste

If using peanut butter: mix with a little water to form smooth paste

Heat in a pot for 2–3 minutes

4. Cook Ndolé

In a large pot, sauté onions (and garlic if using) in palm oil

Add the boiled meat and fish

Stir in the bitterleaf and cook 5–10 minutes

Add the peanut paste gradually, stirring continuously

Adjust consistency with a little water if too thick

Season with salt, pepper, and stock cubes to taste

Simmer for 10 more minutes, stirring occasionally
