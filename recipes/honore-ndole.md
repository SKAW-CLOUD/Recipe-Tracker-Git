# Ndolé (Cameroon Bitterleaf Stew)

## Ingredients

-   2 cups washed bitterleaf
-   1 lb beef or shrimp
-   1 cup ground peanuts
-   1 onion (chopped)
-   2 cloves garlic (minced)
-   Salt & pepper to taste
-   Palm oil

## Instructions

1.  Boil meat until tender.
2.  Add peanuts and cook until thickened.
3.  Stir in bitterleaf and simmer.
4.  Add palm oil, onions, and garlic.
5.  Adjust seasoning and serve warm.
