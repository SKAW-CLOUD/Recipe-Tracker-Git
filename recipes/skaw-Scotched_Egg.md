5. Scotch Egg => By "SKAW"
    Ingredients
* 4 hard-boiled eggs
* Minced meat (beef or sausage)
* 1 raw egg (for coating)
* Bread crumbs
* Salt and pepper
* Oil for frying

    Preparation

* Season minced meat with salt and pepper.

* Wrap each boiled egg with the minced meat.

* Dip in beaten egg, then roll in bread crumbs.

* Deep-fry until golden brown.

* Drain and serve warm.
